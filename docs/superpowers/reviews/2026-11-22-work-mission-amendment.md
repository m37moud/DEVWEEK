# Work missions and separate-date planning

The approved Daymark feature plan adds a Work-only day type for work performed away from the registered place. It amends the planning, history, and reporting behavior in the [frozen contract](2026-10-27-attendance-mvp/01-design-v1-frozen.md) and [promoted design](2026-10-27-attendance-mvp/04-promoted-design-v2.md).

- A mission belongs to one tracked date, including weekly rest days. Eligibility uses the date's Work plan, including frozen past plans. An explicit holiday or vacation and a mission cannot share a date. Remove the conflicting entry before saving the new one.
- An effective mission counts once in elapsed work days and once in elapsed mission totals. It has no confirmed arrival, absence, lateness, or vacation deduction. History lists missions by the selected attendance period, including scheduled missions in that period. Periods spanning Work and another category use the generic Attendance total. Upcoming missions enter totals on their date in the attendance zone.
- Missions preserve GPS evidence, corrections, and any consumed automatic claim. Removing a mission restores the stored outcome or evaluates the date under its original rules. An active mission suppresses location checks and reminder scheduling. Removal does not request retrospective location.
- Plans accept a contiguous range or a set of dates across months. Review and save each batch atomically. Separate-date batches skip dates already covered by the requested kind. Identical ranges are idempotent. Distinct overlapping ranges keep their own entries and charge each eligible date once. Each mission can be edited or removed individually.
- Mission notes are optional and sync as literal text to connected app-owned reports. The report reuses the existing date and note fields. Removal restores the original attendance note and confirmation fields where present. Period attendance totals count missions and Overview provides a dedicated mission count.
